Twenty items, the same twenty a buyer will ask about. For each, three honest states: we have it and can produce the artefact, we have it but cannot prove it, we do not have it. The middle one is the expensive one — it is the state that turns a five-day review into six weeks, and it is invisible on a questionnaire that only has yes and no.

Mark what is true today, not what will be true after the next sprint. Nobody sees this until you copy it and send it. “Cannot prove” is not a confession; it is a work item with a name.
