Exhibitrail  ·  If a buyer forwarded you the checklist

Twenty questions arrived. Here is what each one wants.

Someone on your buyer's side sent you a one-page list of questions, probably with the line “an answer only counts if something can be handed over.” This page translates each question into the artefact behind it — the file, the record, the test — and the role that normally owns it. Nothing here is a requirement invented by us; it is what the question is pointing at.

Read one row as: the question you were asked → the thing that answers it → who usually holds it. If the artefact exists, send it. If it does not, say so and say what would create it. A named gap costs less than a smooth paragraph, because the buyer has to write something in their notes either way.

C1How is a conversation keyed, and can a caller choose the key? The key derivation and the rejection path for a mismatched key, plus one test that proves it. tests/tenant-key.spec platform

D1Retention per data class, and who can shorten it? A schedule: class, period, mechanism, contact. Silence on transcripts is the usual hole. policy/retention.md legal / dpo
D2Is customer content used for training — and what enforces that? The clause, the technical control, and the exception list (abuse review is a common one). legal + platform
D3Do recordings, transcripts and tool payloads follow the same policy? The line in the policy that names them — or an honest “not yet”. legal

E1Which model providers see customer content, on what terms? A dated subprocessor list with purpose and each provider's retention. trust/subprocessors.csv legal / security
E2Is the model version pinned, and who can change it? The pinning mechanism, the change process, and the notice window before a swap. platform
E3What is re-tested after a model change? The last re-test record, scoped to refusals, tool calls and retention behaviour. qa
E4Does the trust centre's list match the contractual one? Both, side by side. They diverge more often than vendors expect. security

If you are missing more than three of these

The usual reason is not negligence. It is that nobody was ever asked to produce them in a form a buyer could read: the checks live in code, the decisions live in a thread, the retention lives in a paragraph written for a different audience. Turning those into three pages of artefact is a two-day job — and it is the difference between a five-day review and a six-week one.

We do that work, and we do it in writing. Send the list of what you cannot hand over; you get back what each one requires, and a scope, within one working day. No call at any stage.
